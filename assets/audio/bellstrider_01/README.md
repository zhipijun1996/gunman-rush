# Bellstrider material audio 01

BELLSTRIDER-REWORK-01 dedicated SFX candidate. Seventeen mono PCM16 / 48kHz WAVs derive solely from preserved CC0 sources: rubberduck's metal, wood, breaking and impact recordings, plus PWL's bell through the exact existing quiet-02 `plains_chime_a.wav` derivative. No commercial game sample, synthesized oscillator/noise bed, BGM edit, or replacement of selected player hurt A is included.

`python3 tools/build_bellstrider_01_audio.py` rebuilds and `--validate` verifies source page, license, original-source and output fingerprints. `sources.json` retains authors, original filenames, URLs, commercial terms, SHA256 and parent bell derivative recipe. `recipes.json` gives exact recorded layers, filtering, playback rate, duration and timing; `manifest.json` records actual output metrics, producer SHA and ffmpeg version. The CC0 legal text is preserved here and at the original source paths.

The service `BellstriderAudio.bind(encounter, origin)` listens only to accepted `presentation_event` dictionaries, using `event` and the encounter `age`/`source_clock`. Root binds it under the actual presentation. Boss warnings use the root-owned `GameAudio.play_boss_cue` API in its existing twelve voices; hit material uses existing `play_enemy_material`. No player or pool is created by this service. `bellstrider_bell_hit` aliases `weak_hit`; metal armour is short and muffled; weak bell is bright and short. A planned return roll emits two audible strikes before its first charge, while the actual later charge keeps a single warning. Each actual ring wave emits exactly its own strike event. Lob aim, lock, release and real impact have distinct events.

Warnings have priority 90; material hits remain priority 45. Critical cues stop earlier body reactions and suppress subsequent 10Hz hit chatter during their short windows. Hits also have 170ms/140ms weak/armour intervals. Death starts by stopping prior tails, followed by committed jam, body impact and final bell events; final bell has a 1.1s tail. Receipts and deduplication memory are bounded. Sound never decides HP, phase, attacks, death, rewards, save or RNG.

Lifecycle: voices are origin owned and pausable through GameAudio. Tree exit/health epoch change cancels the consumer; explicit `cancel()` stops its origin's tails. Encounter `bellstrider_audio_cancel` stops current tails while allowing later accepted events after attack cancellation. No delayed callback or timer can replay an old attack. Root must bind a dedicated origin (the service node is the default), retain the service through the final death tail, and emit cancellation or call `cancel()` on encounter cancellation/unload.
