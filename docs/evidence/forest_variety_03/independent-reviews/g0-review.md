# Independent G0 review: preserve v15, vary actual modules and topology

Reviewer `/root/forest_repeat_diagnosis`; production read-only. Sources read: current new `forest_playful_module_variants.gd`, `thorn_forest_region_v2_v17.gd`, full Forest Bible, v15 producer chain, and direct messages from both implementation authors. No engine was run. This is an early design review, not Motor/art/FUN/device acceptance. The layout author has not yet completed the new layout file; all geometry analysis below is source-based risk, not a measured runtime failure.

## Decision

**CONTINUE only the two limited connection goldens; broader eight-module/two-map satisfaction UNVERIFIED.** Preserving v15 and stopping the v16 auto-ladder generator addresses the user's latest direction. The initial two factories do not yet establish new module variety or random maps. Do not expand them into six mandatory repeats before checking actual movement and normal-scale composition.

## G0-01: two first goldens are one geometry family

Current `forked_bough_hh` and `side_root_rh` derive old HH/RH phrases but both now have the same high crown rectangle[280,-112,320,24], goal[355,-130], lower_exit[720,0], outer support[650,18,260,32], broad recovery, one western lip and equivalent upward/outer-descending topology. The explicit airborne programs differ. Both authors acknowledge they are connection variations, not two independently established novel gameplay families.

**Cheapest strategies to compare:**

- HH: walk to the legal right departure lip, full held jump with right input, cast directly to second fruit[330,-170]. If this works, the first fruit[200,-40] is optional; two accepted H events alone do not establish the intended hand-off's value.
- RH: full held jump plus direct H to[300,-170], omitting initial R. Also test held jump with one real R directly to the broad crown, omitting H. Check aim ray and accepted contact, not requested input letters.
- Both: take high crown then walk/drop outward; compare the direct lower route from departure or basin. The world must bind crown and lower_exit to different destinations. If both reach the same next module with no material resource/time/risk/reward tradeoff, the secondary port is a formal branch.
- Deliberately miss final H and verify a complete ordinary recovery to departure with actual resources. Its catch cannot intercept an adjacent module's flight or lie over the old v15 landing.

Do not outlaw successful alternatives to force a combo. If both modules reduce to the same best jump/H decision, retain them as compatible shape variants and get variety elsewhere.

## G0-02: new route may remain a single long corridor

The planned new main path starts near old rootbay y1982 and meets old F05 recovery around[1980,1302], requiring net680wu upward travel. A six-piece horizontal chain distributing that rise still risks repeating the same upward judgement; old fast/high line plus one slower new line supplies only one macro loop.

**Required before broad claims:** at least one module with an actual high/low destination split, one path crossing to a different known-root destination, and a reward/return with a different exit. These need not all be mandatory. Explicitly list which physical route destinations change per seed. A sampled sequence containing different module IDs is not enough if all instances remain left-in/right-out serial.

Preserve original F05 approach drop[1630,1182]→[1980,1302], standing body and actual support. New one-way support may not overlap its solid at the same height and take over floor contact. Do not put a new support between the original deep-descent and its receiver, or over a root so close that respawn body overlaps its underside.

## G0-03: reused modules are useful, but not new mechanisms by count

Author's current shortlist: two HH/RH connector variants, rooted double window, descending thorn elbow, broken-bough backside, live-pod choice, and older arch/bridge phrases. Old v15 already has real double windows with weaver, F05 backside and F08 deep-root choreography. These should be preserved and can form materially different new combinations; they cannot be described as eight new fun modules merely because they have eight catalogue IDs.

A meaningful new variant can reuse old movement but must change a judgement: a double window whose outlets lead to different destinations, a down-first elbow returning behind a known root, or an enemy/fruit/shore interaction that changes which branch is worth taking. Validate actual world connections and choices. Classify delivery as preserved-old, connection-variant, new-shaped-challenge and new-interaction, without inventing a numerical FUN score.

## G0-04: genuine seed variation needs source consumers

Old v13 first_inner changes recommendation only; both physical branches stay. New production must not repeat that mistake. For two or more seeds, compare normalized actual platform/fruits/hazards, admitted enemies, opened-door relation and branch destination adjacency. Show an example where a player standing on the same recognizable root has a different real route option, not just a different route-array order or label.

Layout rotations that alter neither the purpose of a gap nor the destination after it count as geometry variation, not automatically meaningful topology. Physical diversity, expert design review, actual Motor, art and user/device acceptance remain separate.

## First two whole-world goldens

A. New opening: ordinary fullspawn→first1H observation→chosen lower/side branch→new crown→old root. Compare intended H/R routes, direct legal alternatives and one complete failure/retry. This must show an easier readable opening while the good old long chain remains optional.

B. Actual fork/rejoin: from one broad known root choose high window to one continuation or low window to a different return/reward approach; take real OR reward and return via a different mouth. Compare skipping reward, cheapest direct route, and a second seed where actual joining destination/support set changes. If new geometry merely places a side reward on another upper shelf, REWORK before broad expansion.
