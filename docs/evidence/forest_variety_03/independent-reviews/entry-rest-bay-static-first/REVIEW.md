## What should be checked before accepting this change

1. Actual fullspawn lesson→first rest H→second H, with real target IDs, complete12-frame stable rest, unchanged charges/resources and no hidden corrective shot.
2. Original continuous interwoven HH and original optional root_spiral H-H-R-H under the new neighbour geometry, including actual guide/capture rays; not only unchanged entity hashes.
3. A missed first/second H reaching existing safe roots and a real retry, without constructing a new ladder.
4. Normal-scale actual composition: the new limb is visibly part of the old tree, not a floating short-platform stack or a surface hiding fruit/thorns. No screen evidence has yet been supplied for this helper.
5. Route records: standard routes may choose the resting split in v17, while legacy routes retain their identity. A new physical branch may legitimately change timings and recovery, so do not claim old trajectories remain byte-valid merely because the old generator is retained.

Bigger map, more module mechanisms, random seed variety and art approval remain separate work. This single early rest does not satisfy those broader requests. Public restoration to the validated old package is a separate delivery action; this static review does not independently re-verify its PCK or GUI smoke.
