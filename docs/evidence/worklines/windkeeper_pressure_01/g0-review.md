## Concrete design risks which must be falsified in implementation

1. **PhaseIII bridge change needs a live safe exit.** Original arena removes ground x290..1000 and retains real shelves x290..470,550..730,810..1000 at y470. Their80/80wu gaps are promising for the unchanged singlejump/recoil rules, but base ground y600 is130wu lower. A phase change while firing on ground or beside a shelf must provide enough visible transition time to reach real support before floor removal; no forced environmental hit. Inspect both shores, centre-floor and under-shelf states. Old safe-anchor coordinates are recovery behavior, not proof that immediate transition is safe.
2. **Gust→sweep/seeds cannot reserve the sole receiving edge twice.** Frozen composition is necessary but does not prove fairness. At each actual current platform, test leftward and rightward wind, low resources, and player standing near either edge. The next attack should leave a genuinely reachable safe side/height, including the swept volume and any prior still-live projectile. Default ground movement260 and original local wind≤80 do not themselves prove the full sequence can be evaded.
3. **Lock semantics must be real.** Lunge heading fixed before takeoff; no air steering toward the player. Platform aim must name a real support with a stable target chosen in preparation, not a bare player coordinate that can become a fake floor. Follow-up warning may track early but needs its own fixed commitment before contact. Selecting a group before first warning must not change attack count or hidden completion condition after damage/phase crossing.
4. **'Recover' must mean the group really ended.** No unannounced delayed seed, impact or gust may be spawned during recovery. Prior projectiles can remain dangerous only if explicitly visible and included when measuring two shots and retreat. Neither nominal recoveryseconds nor two accepted Router requests proves two real hits plus safe exit. Record actual evasion complete→grounded shot1→shot2 recoil→retreat→next threat arrival. An optional third shot must reduce real time/space margin, not invoke an artificial hit or bespoke punish.
5. **Pose equality has to include damaging extremities.** Four feet/long forelimb/blade endpoints and physical lunge movement need one authoritative pose. Rotated visual shoulders or a far-stretched foot do not justify a target-centred fabricated hit. Body-contact geometry must cover actual solid material while preserving visible clear air. Old current armour/continuous-body damage rules and legitimate target-bound hook protection must stay separate.
6. **Pressure is a bounded reaction, not a hidden aim rule.** Camping pressure should be established by current position/time/history; weighted choice may vary but already-telegraphed actions remain trustworthy. PhaseIII should select current/reachable support pressure rather than cycle predetermined piers. A repeated single-action legal strategy should be observed as a control comparison; no claim that randomization alone proves depth or eliminates every cheap strategy.
7. **Terminal safety and reward ownership remain atomic.** Death disables active gust/contact/all owned attacks immediately, while the1.8–2sec pose clock still completes. Existing gold/item chain and discrete coin values remain once-only. Sprayed coins must choose physical reachable shelf support after the bridge is broken, not the removed floor or the boss sprite's apparent feet. Same-frame player death and boss defeat preserve existing failure-before-reward policy.
