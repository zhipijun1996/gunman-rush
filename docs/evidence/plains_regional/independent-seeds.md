## Pixel finding across actual traversal

**The route shows real breadth, not a three-place chain.** Viewed windows include creek/reward/shortcut, bell encounters, deep ascending/descending sections, lower return and final shelter. Actors, enemies, saws, heart and coin objects coexist with the new terrain. Airborne and running girl silhouettes are generally separable in the viewed settled windows; no claim of universal readability or action-state coverage is made.

**Art remains failed across that breadth.** Long shelves, narrow pillars, hanging stone blocks and repeated identical vine bridges dominate. Foreground stone remains a continuous global material over orthogonal source geometry rather than integrated designed landforms. The same low-contrast wall band repeatedly cuts through the view as a near-horizontal background horizon, including2400/8400/9600/12000; sky and isolated pale hill recur in the other windows. Deep material shows mirrored rock facets and abrupt stretched bands. Seeing the actual route expands the scope of the observed problem; it does not reverse native02.

### Worst observed local window

**Frame8400 /140s**: the right-hand deep wall exposes an obvious straight horizontal material discontinuity with vertically smeared core below/around the ledge. Its shape is an angular vertical block with rectangular notch; repetitive foreground and smooth background band make depth read as stacked cutouts. This is an asset-coordinate/face grammar failure combined with inherited orthogonal mass geometry, not an insufficient decoration count. Girl is actually visible at this frame; do not misreport it as actor occlusion.

Frame10800/180s separately shows large squared hanging blocks above a long flat hazard floor and repeated diagonal stone below. Frame7800/130s demonstrates the counterexample: the player is clear and the vine walkway is physically readable against sky/hill, yet a very long unsupported repeated vine segment still lacks the intended plains spatial identity. Technical readability can succeed while art design fails. Final frame12494/208.233333s shows actual shelter feet/support, but a single good local attachment does not rescue global landform composition.
