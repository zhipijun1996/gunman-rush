## Why the second method still fails

**Primary causal layer: macro embedding and regional landform generation.** In the whole-world view, deeper solids become tall vertical rectangular columns joined to horizontal shelves. The cuts between them remain rectangular wells; suspended upper modules remain hanging slabs or pillars. Turning every support into a deeper body preserves the orthogonal route grid rather than designing a landscape around traversable space. An actual hill/ridge/creek system has not emerged. Native correctness of added supports does not make this coherent geology.

**Asset grammar:** repeated mirrored rock facets form obvious alternating vertical patterns in the largest bodies. The normal entry right face still shows strongly stretched/linear segments around its underside transition. One geological painting, remapped by nearest-top depth, is being required to explain shoulders, large faces, roots, deep interiors and shaft walls; it does not have the directional/scale variants needed for those roles. Adding another global texture would not fix the initial orthogonal mass plan.

**Global composition and depth:** the middle distance still resolves into horizontal bands, now quieter, while the sky occupies much of the opening view. The overview has no convincing hierarchy of one dominant landform, nested secondary masses, regional landmark and intentional distinct voids. Shelter sprites and a tree remain local attachments to route shelves. A continuous coordinate space has not become a unified designed landscape.

## Decision

**REJECT whole-region art; RETHINK macro embedding / landform representation. Stop expanding this method.** Two different representation attempts have actual visual evidence and neither reaches the target. Do not spend another iteration rounding the same columns, multiplying dressing or changing the material period. Retain formal full-region graph/state/contracts, exact boundary/support and consumer checks, but do not call their successful execution artistic acceptance.

The critical replacement experiment is to derive walkable edges and route embedding *within a deliberately structured regional mass-and-void field*, then assign constrained challenge phrases to those edges. Existing full graph can supply exploration relations; its row/column coordinates need not remain the immutable shape of the world. A grounded ridge with a crossable cut and an actual traversable side/underpass would discriminate this from yet another shelf reskin. Its feasible terrain/structure family must be designed together with that field and tested at normal camera and whole-map scale before any full-region expansion.

This is an identified missing production capability, not a recommendation to discard original Motor/resources/save or modify forest. P1 style approval remains with user. Source-bound route movies may establish real full-region technical playability, but they should be labelled failed-art candidate evidence if these defects remain. Formal pool, whole-region golden, stable high-quality random art and market/user/device approval are all **not granted**.
