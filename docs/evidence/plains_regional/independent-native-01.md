## Observed failure, by production layer

**R1 — Regional landform / geometry-to-art representation.** The whole-world image reads as long, disconnected horizontal slabs, thin stair fragments and rectangular vertical towers. The normal entry is two isolated rectangular blocks with lightly rounded lower corners. Stone pigment does not turn those slabs into continuous hills, connected rock masses, eroded shoulders or believable roots. Exact collision union is a useful foundation, but it is not itself the desired visible geology. This is not solved by adding more grass, normal maps or rock texture.

**R2 — Global composition / scene-layer grammar.** Three near-horizontal strata repeat the same enlarged stone wall behind the foreground. They occupy the lower view as flat bands, while the top half of the normal view is empty pale sky. Their shared pigment motif and parallel silhouette collapse the difference between distance layers. A global world phase prevents seams but does not create a composed horizon or spatial depth.

**R3 — Asset grammar / actual image mapping.** The distant civilization appears in the overview as one conspicuous rectangular illustrated card floating above the world. Its hard picture bounds and clouds belong to a self-contained image, not a naturally integrated far-layer silhouette. Merely placing one instance avoids repetition but does not solve integration. Normal start also shows a vertical smearing region at the right/lower stone face; the overview repeats streaked lower belts. Current fullpainting remap exhausts its expressive range at deep faces.

**R4 — Mesoscopic space.** Architectural assets stand on several slabs, but the region still presents stacked route shelves and isolated props, not landforms that enclose, support, reveal or connect those activities. Sparse landmark metadata and several broad quiet rectangles have not become distinctive sequences of open meadow, narrow ridge, creek, shelter and return view.

## Decision and next discriminating experiment

**REJECT regional art; RETHINK the volume and scene-layer method before artistic expansion.** Shared geometry, actual formal topology and world-space coordinates are genuine engineering progress and should be retained. Do not accept more Seeds or more complete-route assertions as substitutes for this failed visual target.

The next experiment must change *representation*: derive regional geological masses from route supports while preserving required shafts/gaps and all visible landing semantics; connect or root surfaces that should belong to one terrain body; give necessary detached challenge platforms an intentional structural identity. Near/far layers need independently designed silhouettes and restrained distinct painting rather than the same cliff sheet expanded in three bands. Remove the floating picture-card presentation. Test at least one old module boundary, a vertical shaft/return and a wide rest view at normal camera plus the same whole-world view. Reject if it merely adds texture/decorations or rounds the same rectangles.

P1 remains user-preferred candidate, not user-approved final art. This judgment concerns actual two viewed images only; it does not claim all windows were inspected or that the art direction itself is impossible. No formal pool/whole-region golden/commercial maturity approval.
