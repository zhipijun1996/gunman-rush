## Fallen — reject current mother shape

Both inside images show the same dominant mechanical structure: a tall narrow left column joins a very long level floor in a right angle; another broad level roof/floor band frames the room. Both cache images invert this into a huge right pillar with a perpendicular shelf. Rounded edge pixels do not change the resulting L bracket. It reads as a constructed rectangular platform chamber rather than the interior/underside of fallen timber. Repeating this same frame in both maps amplifies the problem.

Keep the useful action surfaces and headroom, but replace the surrounding mass: use a single bent or slanting log body with a tapered belly, a clear broken end and a fork/root attachment. Let the upper silhouette and lower silhouette follow different continuous curves instead of parallel slab edges. The cache should occupy an irregular hollow/broken lip in that body; the pillar should lean and broaden into the log rather than meet a floor as an isolated post. Adjust non-playable mass first, preserving the exact receiving/launch tops and doorway clearance; any changed physical union still needs fresh native verification. Uniform bark overlays or small corner cuts cannot fix this failure.

## Z — preserve spatial idea; local geometry still unresolved

The descending pocket and lower cache show a useful change of direction and open negative space. The cache's continuous curved left root makes a substantially better body than the Fallen bracket. Entry gives a readable separation between previous support, current support, lower slope and fruit.

However, both pocket images contain a roughly 500px thin perfectly horizontal arm on a straight vertical post: a drawing of a cantilever rather than a grown branch. Crown has a long flat overhead slab terminating in a detached vertical hanging block, effectively another inverted L. Replace the thin arm with a tapered branch underside sweeping into its parent body without adding collision above its launch surface; tie or reshape the hanging end as a hooked root/branch split instead of a squared pendant. Preserve the open air pocket. This is limited continue-for-refinement, not complete ART approval. The two seeds look almost identical at these local stations; screenshots cannot substantiate major local variation.

## Back — differentiated action layout; material/body shape incomplete

The high cache to the left, suspended fixed fruit to the right and lower starting branch communicate a returnward high reward. Entry/crown preserve welcome open air and do not repeat Fallen's enclosing giant post. This is a distinct spatial setup worth retaining.

Yet the high cache support and ordinary supports remain floating long flat wedges; rounded undersides alone do not establish how they grow or attach. West_entry foreground reads as stacked horizontal masses. Failure shows a nearly screen-wide flat recovery floor beneath another long parallel beam, with little rooted structure. Keep the large safe receiving surface where function requires it, but make its visible belly follow an attached oblique root and a broken/forked silhouette; do not merely put a bark texture over the same two slabs. Back can continue as a gameplay prototype, not an approved natural-branch art template. Its fruit is fixed; no living-fruit behavior is proven here.

## Limits

Static color/family and player/reward readability are present; the problematic shape is not explained away by technical pass. No normal-speed interaction video, audio, touch/device comfort, excitement, global map diversity, or 8–12/15–20 minute timing was assessed. Existing all33 timeout remains failure; these images do not retroactively pass unrun reward/recovery cases. No whole forest ART/FUN or release approval is issued.
