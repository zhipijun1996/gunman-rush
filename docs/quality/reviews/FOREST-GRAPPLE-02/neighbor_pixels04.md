Frames07–08/time5.333–5.833s: the solid trunk overhead, upper/downward red thorn patch and lower/upward thorn patch bound a clearly visible central gap. Sampling does not establish how much real-time observation a novice gets before entering or whether camera interpolation hides an intermediate boundary.

Frames09–10 show below-arch travel and later ascent, with visible next rings, receiving branches and a long lower red thorn band. The thorn band and enormous rectangular trunk masses still read as prototype geometry; scene-material labeling does not make them convincing forest forms. This is a retained visual-quality limitation, not a request for an unsolicited asset rewrite during the current physics freeze. Frame01 is the concrete new repair, while commercial forest art remains outside this sample's acceptance.
