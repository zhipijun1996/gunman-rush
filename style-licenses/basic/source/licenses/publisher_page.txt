Make your first game using this small collection of pixel-art tiles, objects and characters.
This
Asset Pack
is recommended for people who are looking to make their first game and need
easy-to-use free assets.
Included Assets:
Level Tilesets for
4 biomes
(Grass, Desert, Snow and Autumn).
Many objects:
Coins, blocks, switches, signs, checkpoints, trampolines...
1 player character
with some simple animations (Idle, Running, Jumping, Falling, Getting Hurt).
1 slime enemy
with some simple animations (Jumping around / Getting jumped on).
A
layer-based background.
